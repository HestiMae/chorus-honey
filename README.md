Mix Chorus Fruit and Honey to ascend to new heights!
</center>

---

Adds two items to the game:

**Chorus Honey**
- This mysterious end concoction contains the power of chorus!
- Brewed with a chorus fruit and honey bottles at a brewing stand
- When consumed, teleports the player to the nearest open space above

**Chorus Honey Block**
- Like honey, but flows up!
- Airborne entities slide up the block on contact
- Crafted with 4 chorus honey bottles
`path/to/config/files` if you have them

---

This is a standalone port of Chorus Honey from [Pollinators Paradise](https://modrinth.com/mod/pollinators-paradise).
